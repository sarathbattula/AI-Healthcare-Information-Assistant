WORLD HEALTH ORGANIZATION (WHO) - FIRST AID BASICS & MINOR INJURY CARE

1. GENERAL FIRST AID PRINCIPLES
First aid is the immediate assistance given to any person suffering from a minor or severe illness or injury, with care provided to preserve life, prevent the condition from worsening, or promote recovery.

2. CARE FOR MINOR BURNS (FIRST-DEGREE BURNS)
- Cool the burn immediately under cool running tap water for at least 10 to 15 minutes.
- Do not apply ice, iced water, butter, or greasy ointments to burns, as this can damage skin tissues further.
- Gently cover the clean area with a sterile non-stick dressing or clean cloth.
- Protect the burned area from pressure and friction.

3. MINOR CUTS AND SCRAPES
- Wash hands before cleaning the wound.
- Rinse the wound gently with clean water or saline solution to remove dirt.
- Apply gentle pressure with a clean cloth or sterile gauze if there is minor bleeding.
- Cover with a sterile bandage to keep dirt and bacteria out.

4. DEHYDRATION & REHYDRATION
- Signs of mild dehydration include dry mouth, dark urine, thirst, and mild dizziness.
- Drink clean water or Oral Rehydration Solution (ORS) slowly in small sips.
- Avoid sugary sodas or caffeinated drinks when experiencing dehydration.

5. WHEN EMERGENCY MEDICAL CARE IS NEEDED
Emergency services must be contacted immediately if a person experiences severe chest pain, breathing difficulty, uncontrollable bleeding, loss of consciousness, or signs of severe shock. In India, call 112 or 108 for immediate emergency assistance.
